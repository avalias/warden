# WARDEN — demo video narration

Six parts, one per scene of `video/index.html`. Total ~3:00.
Durations are from the placeholder TTS run — use them to pace each part.

---

## Part 1 — Hook (~24s)

Every autonomous AI fund has the same fatal flaw: you have to trust the agent. It holds the keys, you trust its own numbers, and its reasoning is off-chain and deniable. WARDEN is the first AI fund you don't have to trust. The agent is powerful — and structurally powerless to do harm. Here's how.

## Part 2 — The inversion (~33s)

The inversion is simple. Every other AI wallet holds a broad key, trusts its own risk numbers, and hides its reasoning off-chain. WARDEN flips all three. Authority is a capability object, never a key. The chain re-derives risk itself, and freezes the vault the moment the agent's claim diverges. And every decision is anchored on a tamper-evident, hash-chained ledger. The agent proposes. The chain disposes.

## Part 3 — The heart (~39s)

Watch the heart of it, live on Sui testnet. The agent proposes a trade and swears its risk is zero — trust me, it says. But the Guardian re-derives the risk on-chain, from a market feed the agent cannot forge — and gets ten thousand basis points. The numbers diverge. The chain doesn't argue. It freezes the vault. A lying, hallucinating, or hijacked agent simply cannot move the fund. And this isn't a slide — it's a real, clickable transaction on Sui.

## Part 4 — It's real (~28s)

None of this is a mockup. Thirteen Move modules. Forty-three out of forty-three tests passing. The full lifecycle anchored on-chain across fifteen clickable transactions — and the package is immutable: the upgrade key has been burned, so not even we can change the leash. A real AI agent proposes the trades, and a live dApp lets you drive the whole thing yourself.

## Part 5 — Seven layers (~34s)

And it goes all the way. Seven composable layers under one thesis: trust-minimization. Non-custodial custody. Object-capability authority. The chain-never-trusts-the-AI core. A self-hedging strategy with provably bounded losses. A hash-chained, verifiable ledger you can re-derive yourself. Compliance gating. And inheritance, so the fund outlives its owner. All built, all on-chain.

## Part 6 — Close (~22s)

WARDEN is the only autonomous AI fund a regulated institution can actually fund — because trusting the agent is no longer required. The chain structurally prevents theft, mis-allocation, and misreporting. Give the machine the markets. Give the chain the leash. That's WARDEN.
