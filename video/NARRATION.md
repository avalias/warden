## Part 1 — Hook  ·  ~20s
*(scene: "Every AI fund makes you trust the agent. WARDEN doesn't.")*

> Every autonomous AI fund has the same fatal flaw: you have to **trust the agent**.
> It holds the keys, you trust its own numbers, and its reasoning is off-chain and
> deniable. WARDEN is the first AI fund you do **not** have to trust. The agent is
> powerful — and structurally **powerless to do harm**. Here's how.

## Part 2 — The inversion  ·  ~28s
*(scene: "The agent proposes. The chain disposes." — ✕ vs ✓ columns)*

> The inversion is simple. Every other AI wallet holds a broad key, trusts its own
> risk numbers, and hides its reasoning off-chain. WARDEN flips all three. Authority
> is a **capability object**, never a key. The chain **re-derives risk itself** and
> freezes the vault the moment the agent's claim diverges. And every decision is
> anchored on a **tamper-evident, hash-chained ledger**. The agent proposes — the
> chain disposes.
