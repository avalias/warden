The inversion

Every "AI wallet" asks you to trust the agent.
WARDEN doesn't.

The AI only proposes. A deterministic, fail-closed Guardian re-derives the truth on-chain and holds the leash. The agent is powerful — and powerless to do harm.

✕ The usual approach

  • ✕Agent holds a broad wallet key — can drain it
  • ✕You trust the agent's own risk numbers
  • ✕Reasoning is off-chain — unverifiable, deniable
  • ✕One key, one agent, no referee
  • ✕No compliance path → no institutional capital

✓ WARDEN

  • ✓Agent holds a non-transferable capability object, not a key — and never the withdraw path
  • ✓Chain re-derives risk on-chain from a keeper-fed feed the agent can't forge — diverge → freeze
  • ✓Every decision — including rejected ones — recorded with a three-proof receipt on an append-only ledger
  • ✓A separable critic capability co-signs — a distinct critic signer in multi-party deployment
  • ✓Protocol-level KYC gate + optimistic oracle → institution-ready
Architecture

Seven layers. One leash.

Each layer is load-bearing under a single thesis — not a feature dump. Hover a layer. Layer 2 is the heart: the chain never trusts the AI.

L0
Onboarding & custody — non-custodial by construction
module: vault
Funds live in a user-owned Vault as a real Balance. The agent steers real capital between idle and deployed (NAV = idle + deployed, always conserved — funds never leave the vault); only the OwnerCap can remove capital, and it works even while the agent is frozen. A reserve floor is always held back. (zkLogin/Enoki onboarding is roadmap.)

L1
Authority is an object, not a key — object-capability
module: policy
A non-transferable capability minted without store (unforgeable, unsellable), scoped by per-tx cap, rolling-window cap, reserve floor and expiry. The hot-potato Trade must be settled through the policy check in the same PTB — over-scope is structurally impossible. A generation revocation lattice: bump the generation and every older policy dies in one transaction.
non-transferable cap (no-store)hot-potato enforcementper-tx + window capsrevocation latticeagent can't withdraw
L2
The chain never trusts the AI♥ heart
modules: guardian · critic
The AI proposes an unsigned intent. A deterministic, fail-closed Guardian re-derives the risk on-chain from a keeper-fed market feed (price + order-book depth) the agent cannot forge. In a danger zone the agent is clamped to the risk-reducing direction only; fills above a hard ceiling are rejected. A separable critic capability co-signs a digest derived on-chain from the trade. If the chain's re-derivation diverges from the agent's claim → the vault freezes; only governance can unfreeze.

L3
Structured-product engine — it hedges itself
module: strategy
A hedged-carry position (HedgedCarry) earns carry over time and holds a downside hedge. Below the strike the hedge payout offsets further spot losses, so the maximum drawdown is bounded by construction — verified by unit test, and the Settled event carries both the realized magnitude and the drawdown floor on-chain.
hedged-carry positionbounded drawdown (proven)settled from one price readauditable Settled event
L4
Everything is verifiable — memory, reasoning, provenance
module: ledger
Every decision is appended to a monotonic, no-delete, keccak hash-chained ledger — you can't drop or forge an entry without breaking the chain — and each trade digest is replay-protected. Each entry carries a three-proof receipt: the Sui tx digest (real), plus Walrus-blob and TEE-attestation placeholder fields. Rejected attempts are recorded too.

L5
Compliance & confidentiality — institution-ready
modules: compliance · oracle
A closed-loop KYC registry (regulated value moves only between verified holders, enforced at protocol level) with an auditor capability for read-only oversight. A Move-native optimistic oracle resolves real-world outcomes with a challenge window: propose with a bond, dispute while open, finalize when it closes, escalate to a resolver. (Confidential Transfers + Seal threshold-IBE are roadmap.)

L6
Continuity · governance · distribution
modules: inheritance · prize
Dead-man-switch primitive: a heartbeat keeps a position yours; after a dormancy period the named beneficiary inherits control. A commit-reveal draw primitive (keccak256) over a principal counter. (Unit-tested + on-chain; hardening — sharing the switch for third-party claim, a VRF-fair real-Coin draw, x402 + MCP — is roadmap.)

The mechanisms

Load-bearing, end to end.

Every shipped mechanism maps to a Move module that implements it — the three marked roadmap are labeled as such.

🔒

Capital the AI can't take

User-owned vault + non-transferable capability object + a no-withdraw boundary. The agent touches positions, never the exit.

module: vault · policy
⛓️

Risk re-derived on-chain

The Guardian never trusts the agent's number — it recomputes risk from a keeper-fed market feed the agent can't forge, and freezes on divergence.

module: guardian
⚖️

Two agents, one referee

A separable critic capability co-signs each verdict — a distinct critic signer in the intended multi-party deployment. The chain is the final judge.

module: critic
📈

It hedges itself

A self-hedging carry position: below the hedge strike the payout offsets further losses, so the maximum drawdown is bounded by construction.

module: strategy
🧩

Forkable strategies

Compile NL / visual / YAML through a typed Financial IR, price via zero-gas devInspect, mint a composable on-chain strategy object.

status: roadmap
🔏

Proof of every action

Three-proof receipts: the Sui digest is real; the Walrus + TEE fields are placeholders today (real proofs on the roadmap). Rejected attempts are recorded too.

module: ledger
📜

A track record you can't fake

Append-only, non-backdated via Sui Clock, monotonic with no delete path. You can't quietly drop your losing trades.

module: ledger
🏛️

Compliance, enforced

A closed-loop KYC registry keeps regulated value moving only between verified holders, with an auditor capability for read-only oversight.

module: compliance
🛰️

Real-world facts, resolved

Ambiguous real-world outcomes resolve via a Move-native optimistic oracle with a bonded challenge window and a resolver escalation path.

module: oracle
🔐

Private where it must be

Confidential Transfers hide amounts; Seal threshold-IBE + enclave attestation keep risk models encrypted yet verifiable.

status: roadmap
♾️

It outlives its depositor

Dead-man-switch inheritance: a heartbeat keeps a position yours; after a dormancy period it passes to a named beneficiary.

module: inheritance
💸

Agents pay agents

An x402 rail where other agents buy signals per-query; the fee is carved as a second output — the balance change is the receipt.

status: roadmap

Technical depth
51/51 tests · 15 txs

Object-capabilities with hot-potato enforcement, a revocation lattice, on-chain risk re-derivation against an oracle feed the agent can't forge, a dual-key critic, a proven bounded-drawdown, a Move-native oracle, a dead-man-switch — composed under one thesis, deployed and proven on-chain.
